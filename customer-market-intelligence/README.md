# customer-market-intelligence/

Customer behavior and broader market intelligence the agent gathers to inform growth
decisions.

The shape one customer segment research record conforms to (segment definition, needs,
problems, buying motivations, objections, preferences, market, evidence, confidence) is
defined in
[`agent/core/customerSegmentResearchModel.js`](../agent/core/customerSegmentResearchModel.js).
Schema only — no customer research is invented, and confidence defaults to
`unassessed`: an assumption is never claimed as a fact.
